Learning to bid under a budget

Each round every advertiser submits a whole bid vector over the users, with Σj bij ≤ Bi. The highest bid on each user wins and pays its bid (first price). A click is worth v = 5 and happens with a probability fij that the learners never see. They only observe clicks on users they won, and the highest competing bid on every user.

How the agents bid
  • Knapsack best response. Winning user j costs c−i,j+1. With an estimate f̃j, item j is worth f̃jv − c−i,j − 1 and weighs c−i,j+1; a 0–1 knapsack DP over budget Bi picks the users to buy.
  • UCB uses f̃j = min(1, sj/Nj + √(α ln t / Nj)), and 1 for users never won. Round 1 is a zero-bid probe.
  • Thompson sampling draws f̃j ~ Beta(1+sj, 1+Nj−sj).
  • Greedy uses sj/Nj with no bonus; ε-greedy buys a random feasible set with probability 0.1.
  • Known-f best response knows f and best-responds to last round's bids. It isolates what concurrent adaptation does on its own, without any learning.
